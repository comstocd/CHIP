# CHIP Research Project — Document Repository

**David Comstock, MD · Department of Internal Medicine · Guthrie Lourdes Hospital**

Repository of scholarly documents, manuscript drafts, and research planning materials for an ongoing clinical research program in clonal hematopoiesis of indeterminate potential (CHIP), cardiovascular risk, and the inflammaging phenotype in the rural agricultural population of the Guthrie catchment area.

🔗 **Live index:** [https://comstocd.github.io/chip-research/](https://comstocd.github.io/chip-research/)

---

## Project Overview

This research program originates in graduate thesis work in molecular aging biology and stem cell science, subsequently developed through clinical training in internal medicine. The foundational work covering aging-driven stem cell dysfunction, the inflammaging framework, and the biological mechanisms linking cellular aging to malignant predisposition provided the scientific grounding to recognize clonal hematopoiesis as its most directly translatable clinical expression.

The convergence of that academic foundation with the patient population at Guthrie (rural elderly agricultural workers with high rates of unrecognized hematologic abnormality and cardiovascular risk) has produced a research program that is both scientifically grounded and immediately actionable in this clinical setting.

## Research Design

### Scientific Questions

1. **Care gap:** What proportion of Guthrie patients aged 65 and older with CBC patterns consistent with CCUS have received no further hematologic workup, and does the gap differ between the 65-69 and 70-plus age strata?
2. **Prevalence:** What is the prevalence of CHIP driver gene mutations in this rural agricultural cohort versus published urban biobank rates?
3. **Score development:** Can a clinically accessible inflammaging composite derived from routine laboratory values already in the chart predict CHIP genotype and CCUS-level hematologic findings?

### Phased Design

**Phase 1 — Retrospective LIS Query (no external funding required)**
- Retrospective cohort identification via laboratory information system query
- Two-tier flagging: primary (MCV >100 fL + hemoglobin below threshold + normal B12/folate); secondary (MCV 95-100 fL with downward trend; RDW >14.5%; normocytic unexplained anemia)
- Age stratification: 65-69 vs. 70 and above (pre-specified)
- Primary outcome: care gap proportion by tier and age stratum
- IRB: minimal risk retrospective chart review
- Direct cost: near zero

**Phase 2 — Prospective Pilot with Confirmatory NGS (small grant required)**
- Prospective enrollment of Phase 1-flagged patients
- Peripheral blood CHIP next-generation sequencing panel
- Target enrollment: 20-30 patients Year 1
- Estimated cost: $6,000-18,000
- Funding targets: departmental pilot, PAMED Foundation, ASH Research Collaborative
